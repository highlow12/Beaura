# Choose a distribution for the situation

Consider a binomial distribution when counting successes, as in “exactly k successes,” and a normal distribution for continuous values such as measurement error or height. Independence and a constant success probability are key assumptions of the binomial model.

A normal distribution is symmetric around its mean; as σ increases, the curve becomes wider and lower. Do not confuse discrete probabilities with continuous densities, and remember that the probability of any single value in a continuous distribution is 0.
