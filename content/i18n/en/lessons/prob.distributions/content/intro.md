# Probability models for repeated experiments

A Bernoulli distribution models one trial with two outcomes, such as success and failure. If the probability of success is p, then X takes the value 1 or 0 and E[X]=p.

A binomial distribution models the number of successes in n independent Bernoulli trials. A normal distribution, by contrast, describes continuous values with a bell-shaped curve whose location and spread are set by the mean μ and standard deviation σ.
