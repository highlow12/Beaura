# Meaning of the Derivative and Basic Calculations

The definition of the derivative is f′(a)=limₕ→₀ [f(a+h)−f(a)]/h. The numerator is the change in output, and the denominator is the change in input. Finding the derivative f′(x) gives the instantaneous slope at different values of x.

The derivative of a constant function is 0, and the derivative of f(x)=x² is 2x. A positive derivative indicates an increasing function on an interval, while a negative derivative indicates a decreasing function.
