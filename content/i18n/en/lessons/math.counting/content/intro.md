# Two Rules for Counting Cases

The sum rule adds the number of outcomes for several non-overlapping cases. When choosing either `A` or `B`, the two cases must not occur at the same time to avoid double counting.

The product rule multiplies the number of choices at each step when several steps are performed in sequence. If there are `a` choices in the first step and `b` in the next, there are `a×b` ordered choices in total.
