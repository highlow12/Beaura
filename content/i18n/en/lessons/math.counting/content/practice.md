# Distinguishing Overlapping Cases from Sequential Steps

Use the product rule to choose one drink and one dessert from a menu. In contrast, use the sum rule when the choice paths are mutually exclusive, such as choosing either a bus or a subway for one trip.

If cases overlap, you cannot simply add their counts. You may need the inclusion-exclusion principle to subtract the shared part, so first use a diagram or table to check whether cases can happen together.
