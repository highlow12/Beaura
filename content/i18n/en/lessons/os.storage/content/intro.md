# HDDs, SSDs, and Block Devices

To preserve more data than fits in memory, you need to understand persistent storage and its units of access.

A block device is a storage interface that reads and writes fixed-size blocks. HDD performance is dominated by spinning platters and head movement, while SSDs use flash cells and controllers.

In this lesson, we will follow which resources an operating-system abstraction hides and where it enforces boundaries and checks.
