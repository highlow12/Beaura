# Pipelines and Hazards

If the CPU waits for one instruction to finish before starting the next, many of its circuits sit idle.

A pipeline divides execution into stages such as fetch, decode, and execute, and processes different instructions in different stages at the same time. This increases throughput, but does not necessarily reduce the total latency of one instruction.

The goal of this lesson is not just to memorize terms, but to explain how inputs are represented and what hardware path produces the result.
