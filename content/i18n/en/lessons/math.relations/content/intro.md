# A Relation Is a Set of Pairs

The Cartesian product A × B of sets A and B is the collection of ordered pairs (a,b) whose first element comes from A and whose second comes from B. Since order matters, (a,b) and (b,a) are generally different pairs.

A relation R is a subset of A × B. In other words, a relation is a collection of pairs selected as related. Joins between database rows and edges in a graph can also be viewed as relations formed from selected ordered pairs.
