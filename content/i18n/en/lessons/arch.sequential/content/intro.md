# Sequential Circuits and Flip-Flops

In a combinational circuit, the output changes when the input changes, but a CPU also needs circuits that preserve previous state.

A sequential circuit’s output depends on both its current inputs and its internal state. A flip-flop stores one bit of state and accepts a new input on a specific clock edge.

The goal of this lesson is not just to memorize terms, but to explain how inputs are represented and what hardware path produces the result.
