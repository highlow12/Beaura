# Hexadecimal and Bytes

Binary is the computer’s actual representation, but long bit strings are difficult for people to read and compare.

A hexadecimal digit represents one of 16 values from 0 to F and corresponds to exactly 4 bits. Therefore, two digits compactly represent one 8-bit byte.

The goal of this lesson is not just to memorize terms, but to explain how inputs are represented and what hardware path produces the result.
