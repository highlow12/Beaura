## Convert Groups of Four Bits

One hexadecimal digit corresponds exactly to a **nibble**, a group of 4 binary bits. Two nibbles make one 8-bit **byte**. The prefix `0x` marks the following number as hexadecimal.

For example, split `1010 0111` into groups of four bits from the right. `1010` is A and `0111` is 7, so the number is `0xA7`. Conversely, `0x3C` is `0011 1100`. The leading zero pads the bit count and does not change the value.
