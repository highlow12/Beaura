# Routing and the Next Hop

Because a direct link to every destination on the internet is impractical, routers determine the direction to a destination one step at a time.

Routing is the process of calculating a path to a destination network and forwarding packets along it. A routing table can contain destination prefixes, next hops, output interfaces, and costs.

In this lesson, we will follow how packets acquire addresses and state at each layer, and distinguish key networking terms.
