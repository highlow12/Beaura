## Claims and Open Problems

To show that a problem is in NP, provide a fast verification procedure. Saying “every answer can be found quickly” or “it is usually fast on real inputs” does not establish that a problem is in NP or P.

Whether P equals NP remains unproven. Observing that one algorithm is slow, or finding a fast instance of a particular problem, does not establish `P = NP` or `P ≠ NP`; a proof about the classes as a whole is needed.
