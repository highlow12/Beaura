## Θ Is a Bound on Both Sides

Θ(f(n)) is a tight bound: for sufficiently large n, the cost is bounded above and below by constant multiples of f(n). A loop that runs exactly n times is both O(n) and Ω(n), so it is Θ(n).

Bounds can differ depending on whether you analyze the worst, best, or average case, so state the input conditions as well.
