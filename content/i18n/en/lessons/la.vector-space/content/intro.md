# Basis Vectors That Build a Space

A basis of a vector space is a set of vectors that can produce every vector in the space without any vector being redundant as a linear combination of the others. The standard basis `(1, 0), (0, 1)` can produce any `(x, y)` in the plane.

The number of vectors in a basis is the dimension of the space. A space can have many different bases, but every basis has the same number of vectors.
