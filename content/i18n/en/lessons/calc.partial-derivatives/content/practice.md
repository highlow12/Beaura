# Rates of Change by Variable

A partial derivative is local information about change in one direction. For a surface z=f(x,y), the x- and y-direction slopes are found separately and used to construct a tangent plane and the gradient.

Read the mixed partial derivative ∂²f/(∂y∂x) as differentiating first with respect to x, then differentiating the result with respect to y. For sufficiently smooth functions, changing the order of differentiation may give the same result.
