# Vary One Variable at a Time

A multivariable function f(x,y) has two or more inputs. The partial derivative ∂f/∂x finds the rate of change with respect to x alone while holding y constant. For ∂f/∂y, hold x constant instead.

For example, for f(x,y)=x²+3xy, ∂f/∂x=2x+3y. Treat y as a numerical constant, and terms without x become 0 when differentiated with respect to x.
