# A Vector Perpendicular to Both Directions

The 3D cross product `u × v` produces a vector perpendicular to both `u` and `v`. The right-hand rule determines its direction, and its magnitude is `|u||v|sinθ`.

Reversing the order of a cross product changes its sign. Since `u × v = −(v × u)`, keep the order consistent when calculating a normal's direction, a rotation direction, or a face's front and back.
