# Parallelogram Area and Normals

The magnitude of a cross product is the area of the parallelogram spanned by `u` and `v`. A triangle's area is half of that, so it is `1/2 |u × v|`.

If two vectors are parallel, `sinθ = 0`, so their cross product is the zero vector. In graphics, crossing two triangle edges gives a surface normal used in lighting calculations.
