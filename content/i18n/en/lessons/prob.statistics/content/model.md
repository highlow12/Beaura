# Pair Summary Statistics with the Shape of the Data

A single mean or median shows only one aspect of a dataset. Check for outliers and skew, as well as the units and spread, before interpreting a number as “typical.”

The mean of `[30,30,30,30,180]` is `60`, but the median is `30`. Calling 60 the typical spending amount based only on the mean hides that most observations are 30. In this case, it is more appropriate to report the median together with the range or variance.

Common mistakes include assuming that the mean is always representative and that a correlation of 0 means two variables have no relationship. The mean is sensitive to outliers, and correlation summarizes only a particular linear relationship, so consider the data’s shape and context.
