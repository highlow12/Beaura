# Vectors and Coordinates Are Different

A vector is an object in space, while coordinates are a list of coefficients that represent the vector in a chosen basis. The same vector can have different coordinates in a different basis, but its geometric meaning does not change.

In the standard basis, `(x, y)` gives the coefficients of `x e₁ + y e₂`. With a new basis `b₁, b₂`, express the same vector as `c₁b₁ + c₂b₂`, and write its coordinates as `[v]ᵦ = (c₁, c₂)`.
