# Representing a Vector in a New Basis

Place the new basis vectors in the columns of the change-of-basis matrix to connect standard coordinates with new coordinates. Solve the relation `B[c]ᵦ = v` to find the new coordinates `c`.

Coordinate transformations appear in camera coordinates in graphics, axis choices in physics simulations, and feature transformations in data analysis. Mark which coordinates are the input and which are the result.
