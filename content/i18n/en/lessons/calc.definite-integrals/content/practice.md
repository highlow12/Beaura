# Distinguishing Endpoint Evaluation from Area

For ∫₀² x dx, evaluate the antiderivative x²/2 at 2 and 0 to get 2. You can confirm the result using the area of a triangle with base 2 and height 2.

The area between two functions is the integral of the upper function minus the lower function. Before calculating, split the interval at intersection points and determine which function is on top.
