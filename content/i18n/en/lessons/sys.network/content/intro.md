# Connecting Devices and Delivering Data

A computer network is a system in which multiple devices connect through links and exchange data according to defined rules. Devices at either end of communication that produce and consume data, such as laptops and servers, are called **hosts**. Intermediate devices such as switches and routers forward data to the next segment.

Long data is usually split into smaller units called **packets** for transmission. A packet may contain a header needed for delivery and a payload containing the actual content. Not every packet carries ordering or retransmission information; the required control information depends on the protocol and layer.
