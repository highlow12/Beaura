# Continuity and Evaluating Limits

For a function to be continuous at a, f(a) must be defined, limₓ→ₐ f(x) must exist, and they must be equal. A graph with a hole is a common discontinuity where the limit may exist even though the function value is missing or different.

For a function such as a polynomial that allows direct substitution at the point, evaluate the limit by substitution. If you get a 0/0 form, simplify the nearby expression by factoring or canceling, then evaluate again.
