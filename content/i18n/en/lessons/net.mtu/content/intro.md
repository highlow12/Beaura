# MTU and Fragmentation

Each link can carry frames up to a certain size, so an IP packet that is too large may not fit on the next link.

MTU (Maximum Transmission Unit) is the largest complete IP packet, including its header and data, that a link can carry in one frame. The smallest MTU along a path limits the end-to-end transmission size.

In this lesson, we will follow how packets acquire addresses and state at each layer, and distinguish key networking terms.
