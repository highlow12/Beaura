# Clues to Orthogonality and Projection

If the dot product is 0, the vectors are orthogonal. When both vectors are nonzero, this means the angle between them is 90°. Since `(1, 2) · (2, −1) = 2 − 2 = 0`, the two directions are orthogonal.

The dot product also measures how much one vector points in the direction of another. Lighting calculations, similarity between search vectors, and projection all use the dot product.
