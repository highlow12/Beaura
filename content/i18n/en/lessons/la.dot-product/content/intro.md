# Comparing the Directions of Two Vectors

The dot product of two vectors is a scalar found by multiplying corresponding components and adding them. `(a, b) · (c, d) = ac + bd`; the result is a number, not a vector.

Geometrically, the dot product is `|u||v|cosθ`. The angle `θ` is defined when both `u` and `v` are nonzero. A positive dot product means an acute angle, zero means a right angle, and a negative value means an obtuse angle.
