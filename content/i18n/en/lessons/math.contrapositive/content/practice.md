# Simplifying a Proof with the Contrapositive

To form the contrapositive, swap the antecedent and consequent and negate both. Negating only the conclusion or only swapping their order gives the converse or inverse, which are different statements.

A contrapositive proof is especially useful for statements of the form “if one property holds, then another,” when the negated conclusion is easier to handle. At the end, state that the proved contrapositive is logically equivalent to the original statement.
