# Choosing a Rule and Checking Your Work

Use the product rule for an expression written as a product, such as x²sin x, and the chain rule when a function is inside another function, such as sin(x²). You may need both rules at once.

After calculating, check your result by substituting a simple value such as x=0 or checking whether the sign matches what the structure suggests. Common mistakes include omitting one term in the product rule or the inner derivative in the chain rule.
