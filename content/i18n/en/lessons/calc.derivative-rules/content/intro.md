# Differentiating a Complex Expression in Parts

Differentiation distributes over sums, and a constant factor can be carried through unchanged. Since (f+g)′=f′+g′ and (cf)′=cf′, you can handle each term of a polynomial separately.

The product rule is (fg)′=f′g+fg′, and the chain rule for composite functions is (f∘g)′(x)=f′(g(x))g′(x). The key is to identify the outer and inner functions.
