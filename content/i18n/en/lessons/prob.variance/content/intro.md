# How Far Are Values from the Mean?

Variance Var(X) is the average squared distance of X from its expected value E[X]. Var(X)=E[(X−μ)²], where μ=E[X]. Because deviations are squared, variance is nonnegative and its units are the square of the original variable’s units.

A computationally convenient formula is Var(X)=E[X²]−(E[X])². The standard deviation σ is the square root of the variance, so it measures spread in the same units as the original variable.
