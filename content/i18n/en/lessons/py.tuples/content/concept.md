## The Comma Makes a Tuple

In a tuple, the comma matters more than the parentheses. `(7,)` is a tuple with one element, while `(7)` is just the number 7. Separate multiple elements with commas, as in `("Ada", 36)`.

You can read an element with an expression such as `point[0]`, but changes such as `point[0] = 5` are not allowed. If you need a change, create a new tuple and assign it again.
