## Safe Edges

Consider a cut that separates a group of vertices from the rest. The lightest edge crossing that cut is a safe choice that can be included in an MST. This property underlies Kruskal’s and Prim’s algorithms.

An MST does not guarantee the shortest path between every pair of vertices. Distinguish the problem of minimizing the total cost to connect all vertices from the problem of minimizing the path cost between two specific vertices.
