# Boolean Algebra and XOR

When many logic gates are connected, it can be difficult to tell whether the same behavior can be expressed with a shorter formula.

In Boolean algebra, variables are 0 or 1; AND behaves like multiplication, OR like addition, and NOT like complement. XOR produces 1 only when its two inputs differ.

The goal of this lesson is not just to memorize terms, but to explain how inputs are represented and what hardware path produces the result.
