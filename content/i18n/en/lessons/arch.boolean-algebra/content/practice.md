## Check Expressions with a Truth Table

**AND** outputs 1 when all inputs are 1; **OR** outputs 1 when at least one input is 1; **XOR** outputs 1 when its two inputs differ.

If A=1 and B=0, then `A AND B=0`, `A OR B=1`, and `A XOR B=1`. When A=1 and B=1, OR is 1 but XOR is 0. Interpreting XOR as “at least one input is 1” confuses it with OR. If you are unsure about a logic expression, list every possible input combination in a table and compare the outputs.
