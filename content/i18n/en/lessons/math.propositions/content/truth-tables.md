# Logical Operations and Truth Tables

Combining propositions `P` and `Q` lets us form more complex propositions.

- NOT `¬P`: Reverses the truth value of `P`.
- AND `P ∧ Q`: True only when **both** `P` and `Q` are true.
- OR `P ∨ Q`: True when **at least one** of `P` and `Q` is true.

A truth table lists every possible input combination and calculates the result for each. Two propositions have four input combinations: `true-true`, `true-false`, `false-true`, and `false-false`.
