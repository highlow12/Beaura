# Floating-Point Representation

It is difficult to store very large and very small numbers with the same fixed decimal point, so computers use floating-point representation, which is similar to scientific notation.

Floating-point bits are usually divided into sign, exponent, and significand fields. The exponent shifts the decimal point to represent a wide range of values.

The goal of this lesson is not just to memorize terms, but to explain how inputs are represented and what hardware path produces the result.
