# Two’s-Complement Integers

To store signed integers, the bits must distinguish positive from negative values and support continued addition.

In two’s-complement representation, the most significant bit indicates the sign. A negative value is made by inverting the bits and adding 1. The range of n bits is -2^(n-1) through 2^(n-1)-1.

The goal of this lesson is not just to memorize terms, but to explain how inputs are represented and what hardware path produces the result.
