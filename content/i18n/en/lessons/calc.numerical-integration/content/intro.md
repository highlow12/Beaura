# Approximate the Area Under a Curve with Small Shapes

When only function values are given, you can approximate a definite integral by dividing the interval into small pieces and adding the areas of rectangles or trapezoids. A rectangular sum uses the height at one endpoint, while the trapezoidal rule uses the average of the endpoint heights.

For n intervals with uniform spacing h, the trapezoidal sum is h[(f₀+fₙ)/2+f₁+…+fₙ₋₁]. If the curve bends significantly away from a straight line, subdivide the intervals further to reduce the error.
