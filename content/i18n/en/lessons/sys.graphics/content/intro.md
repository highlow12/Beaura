# Two Ways to Represent Images and Scenes

Computer graphics is the field of creating images and screens through computation. It helps to distinguish storing an already rendered image from describing a scene that can be rendered.

A **raster image** divides a screen into a grid of small pixels and stores the color of each pixel. RGB color is usually represented by values for the red, green, and blue channels, which combine to create many colors.

**Vector graphics** and 3D scenes store geometric information such as points, lines, and shapes, then calculate pixel results as needed. Unlike a raster image, which enlarges its existing pixels, shapes can be redrawn at a new resolution.
