# Revealing an Impossible Assumption

A proof by contradiction assumes that the conclusion to be proved is false, then shows that the assumption conflicts with an established fact. A contradiction occurs when two statements that cannot both be true are derived together.

For example, assume that `√2` is rational and write it as a fraction in lowest terms. The argument implies that both the numerator and denominator must be even, contradicting the claim that the fraction is in lowest terms. Therefore, the original assumption is false.
