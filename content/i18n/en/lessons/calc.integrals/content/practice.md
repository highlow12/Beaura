# The Connection Between Differentiation and Integration

The Fundamental Theorem of Calculus says that differentiating an accumulation function gives the original function, and that a definite integral can be calculated as the difference of an antiderivative at the endpoints. This connection turns infinitely many small pieces into a computable expression.

Check the units of the integration variable and interval. The integral of a negative function includes the area below the x-axis with a negative sign, so it may differ from geometric area, which uses absolute values.
