# Accumulating by Adding Small Pieces

Integration adds the contributions of very small pieces to find a total accumulated quantity. Integrating velocity with respect to time gives signed displacement; to find total distance traveled, integrate speed or the absolute value of velocity with respect to time. Integrating density over space can give total mass.

The indefinite integral ∫f(x)dx is the family of functions whose derivative is f, and it includes the constant of integration C. Since C disappears when differentiated, multiple antiderivatives can have the same derivative.
