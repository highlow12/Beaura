# Events can be combined like sets

The union A∪B contains outcomes where A or B occurs, and the intersection A∩B contains outcomes where both occur. The complement Aᶜ contains outcomes where A does not occur.

The probability of a union is P(A∪B)=P(A)+P(B)−P(A∩B). We subtract the intersection once because outcomes shared by A and B were counted twice.
