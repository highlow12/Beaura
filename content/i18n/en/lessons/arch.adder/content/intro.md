# Adders and Addition in the ALU

A one-bit addition produces two pieces of information: the sum for the current bit and a carry to the next bit.

A half adder calculates the sum and carry of two inputs. A full adder also includes the carry-in from the previous bit. Connecting full adders creates a multi-bit adder.

The goal of this lesson is not just to memorize terms, but to explain how inputs are represented and what hardware path produces the result.
