# From Assumption to Conclusion

A direct proof assumes that the statement's hypothesis is true and applies known definitions, theorems, and algebraic rules step by step to reach the conclusion. At each step, state the reason why the next equation or statement follows.

For example, to show that the sum of two even numbers is even, write them as `2k` and `2m`, then simplify their sum to `2(k + m)`. The final expression fits the definition of an even number, completing the proof.
