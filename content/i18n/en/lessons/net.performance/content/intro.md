# Latency, Bandwidth, and Loss

A network can feel slow for different reasons: the round trip may take too long, too little data may fit in a given time, or loss may force retransmissions.

Latency is the time it takes data to reach its destination, and bandwidth is the maximum number of bits that can be delivered per unit of time. Throughput is the rate of data successfully delivered and is affected by loss and congestion.

In this lesson, we will follow how packets acquire addresses and state at each layer, and distinguish key networking terms.
