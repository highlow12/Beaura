## A Quick Choice Does Not Guarantee the Right Answer

With arbitrary coin denominations, choosing the largest coin first may not produce the fewest coins. For example, to make 6 with coins of 1, 3, and 4, choosing 4 first requires three coins, while two 3-value coins are enough.

Therefore, first state the greedy rule and prove that it is safe for every input. If that is difficult to prove, compare the result with DP or exhaustive search to look for a counterexample.
