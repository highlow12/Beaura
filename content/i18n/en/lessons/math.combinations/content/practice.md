# Removing Duplicate Orders from Permutations

A combination is obtained from `nPr` by dividing out all possible orders of the selected `r` items. In other words, `nCr = nPr/r!`. To distinguish permutations from combinations, check whether roles, positions, or rankings are different.

When choosing a team of two from six people, swapping the two people must not count as a new selection. There are `6C2 = 15` teams, but `6P2 = 30` ways to choose a president and vice president.
