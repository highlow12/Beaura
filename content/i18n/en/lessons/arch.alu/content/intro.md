# The ALU and Arithmetic-Logic Operations

The ALU is at the center of turning CPU instructions into calculations, selecting one of several operation circuits.

The ALU is a combinational circuit that performs operations such as addition, subtraction, AND, OR, and comparison. A control signal selects the operation, and the ALU can produce flags such as zero and carry along with the result.

The goal of this lesson is not just to memorize terms, but to explain how inputs are represented and what hardware path produces the result.
