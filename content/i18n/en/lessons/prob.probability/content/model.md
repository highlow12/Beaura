# Choose a Model Before Counting Outcomes

Before calculating a probability, decide what counts as an outcome. You can count outcomes with `|A|/|Ω|` only when each outcome is equally likely. Otherwise, add the probabilities of the individual outcomes.

For two flips of a fair coin, the elementary outcomes are `Ω={HH, HT, TH, TT}`. The event of getting at least one head is `{HH, HT, TH}`, so its probability is `3/4`. Grouping outcomes by the number of heads also describes this event exactly.

A common mistake is to assume that the head counts `0, 1, 2` are equally likely. Their actual probabilities are `1/4, 1/2, 1/4`. The four elementary outcomes are equally likely; the head counts are not.
